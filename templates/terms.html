4. The Zero-Knowledge Architecture

QuantumVault is designed so that all sensitive cryptography — key generation, encryption, and decryption — happens client-side, in your browser, using a hybrid post-quantum scheme (ML-KEM-768 + X25519, combined via HKDF-SHA256, with AES-256-GCM for authenticated encryption) and SRP-6a for password authentication. As a direct consequence:

  • Your password is never transmitted to or stored by the Service in any recoverable form.
  • Your private keys exist on the Service only as a blob encrypted with a key derived from your password; the Service cannot decrypt this blob.
  • Your messages and files are stored and relayed as ciphertext that the Service cannot decrypt.
  • If you lose your password, your encrypted data becomes permanently unrecoverable. There is no backdoor, master key, or recovery mechanism — this is an intentional property of the design, not an oversight.

Operational metadata that is not protected by end-to-end encryption (such as account identifiers, IP addresses in server logs, and message/file timestamps and sizes) may be visible to the Operator as described in the Privacy Policy.
