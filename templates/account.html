Secure notes

Store private notes that are encrypted in your browser before they ever reach the server. Notes are saved under a passphrase; only that passphrase can read them back. You may save a second, independent set of notes under a different passphrase. Notes saved under one passphrase are invisible to anyone who knows only the other, and the stored data looks the same whether or not a second set exists.

Encryption and decryption happen entirely in your browser using PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM, both resistant to quantum attack. Your passphrases and notes never leave this device, and the server cannot tell how many passphrases you have set or what any of them protect. If you forget a passphrase, the notes saved under it cannot be recovered.
