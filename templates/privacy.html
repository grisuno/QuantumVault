Privacy Policy

QuantumVault is free, open-source, zero-knowledge software. This page describes what the software itself collects and, more importantly, what it is structurally unable to access, regardless of the intentions of whoever operates a given instance ("the operator"). Because each instance is independently self-hosted under the AGPLv3, the operator of the instance you are using is the party responsible for its data practices, retention periods, and any applicable local law; this page describes the guarantees built into the software itself, which apply to every instance.

What the Operator Can Never See

  • Your password: authentication uses SRP-6a, a zero-knowledge proof. Your password is never transmitted, in any form, to the server.
  • Your private keys: your hybrid ML-KEM-768 + X25519 key pair is generated in your browser and stored only as a blob encrypted with a key derived from your password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations). The server only ever receives that encrypted blob.
  • Message and file contents: messages and files are encrypted client-side with AES-256-GCM before upload. The server stores and relays opaque ciphertext only.

Information the Software Collects

To provide the service, an instance necessarily stores some information:

  • Account information: the email address (and any other identifier, such as a phone number, if the registration form requires it) used to create and recover access to your account, plus your SRP verifier (a value that proves you know your password without revealing it).
  • Encrypted payloads: your encrypted private key blob, encrypted files, and encrypted messages, all opaque to the server.
  • Public keys: your ML-KEM-768 and X25519 public keys, which are not secret and are needed by other users to encrypt data to you.
  • Operational metadata: standard web server logs (such as IP addresses, timestamps, and request metadata), and message/file timestamps and sizes, which are not protected by end-to-end encryption and may be visible to the operator of an instance.

How This Information Is Used

The data described above is used only to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your session
  • Store and relay your encrypted data so you can retrieve it
  • Operate, secure, and maintain the instance
  • Comply with legal obligations applicable to the operator

Because QuantumVault is AGPLv3, the source code that processes this data is publicly available, so anyone can verify these claims directly rather than relying on a promise.

Your Rights

You may request to view, correct, or delete the account metadata held by an instance (email/identifiers, account status, and stored ciphertext) at any time, by contacting the administrator of that instance. Because content is end-to-end encrypted, deleting your account and its stored ciphertext is equivalent to deleting your data — no one, including the operator, can decrypt it afterward. No instance sells your data, and the software contains no mechanism for monetizing or sharing your data with third parties.

Changes to This Policy

This page describes the privacy properties of the QuantumVault software itself and is updated alongside the software. Operators of individual instances may publish additional, instance-specific privacy information (for example, their jurisdiction, log retention period, or contact details) separately.
